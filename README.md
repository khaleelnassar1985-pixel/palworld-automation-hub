## 🌟 Overview

Welcome to **PalCompanion Suite**, a reimagined automation framework crafted for players who want to spend less time micromanaging and more time exploring the wilds of Palworld. Where the original concept focused on raw scripting, this project takes a stride further: it delivers a modular, extensible, and community-driven toolkit that turns repetitive chores into a background hum rather than a daily grind.

Think of it as a silent caretaker for your base — one that never sleeps, never complains, and always keeps your Pals fed, your resources stocked, and your production lines humming while you're off taming legends in the far reaches of the map.

The suite is built on a philosophy of *quiet efficiency*: automation should feel invisible. You configure it once, and it fades into the rhythm of your gameplay, surfacing only when you need to check a report or tweak a strategy.

---

## 🎯 Why PalCompanion Suite Exists

Palworld is a game of staggering scale. Between base management, resource gathering, breeding chains, and combat expeditions, the sheer number of tasks can overwhelm even the most dedicated player. PalCompanion Suite was born from a simple realization: **your time is the rarest resource in the game**.

Instead of treating automation as a blunt instrument, this project treats it as a craft. Each module is designed with intent, respecting the game's spirit while removing friction. The result is a toolkit that feels less like a script collection and more like a trusted lieutenant.

## ✨ Feature Highlights

- **🧠 Adaptive Task Scheduling** — The scheduler learns your play patterns over time, prioritizing tasks that matter most during your active hours and deferring low-priority chores to idle windows.
- **🌍 Multilingual Support** — Interface and log outputs available in English, Spanish, French, German, Japanese, Korean, and Simplified Chinese, with community-contributed translations expanding regularly.
- **📱 Responsive Interface** — Whether you're monitoring from a widescreen desktop or checking in from a compact tablet, the control panel adapts fluidly to your viewport.
- **🕐 Around-the-Clock Assistance** — The suite is engineered for continuous operation, with graceful recovery from interruptions and detailed health reporting so you always know its status.
- **🔧 Modular Architecture** — Enable only the modules you need. Every component is self-contained, so you can build a lean setup or a comprehensive one without compromise.
- **📊 Rich Analytics Dashboard** — Visualize resource flow, Pal productivity, and breeding outcomes over time. Spot bottlenecks before they become problems.
- **🧬 Breeding Blueprint Planner** — Chart multi-generation breeding paths toward target traits, with probability estimates and suggested pairings.
- **🏗️ Base Layout Optimizer** — Receive suggestions for workstation placement that minimize travel time and maximize throughput.
- **🛡️ Safe-Mode Execution** — A conservative default profile that performs only non-invasive operations, ideal for players who prefer a cautious approach.
- **🔔 Event-Driven Notifications** — Get alerted when rare Pals appear, when raids are detected, or when a production queue stalls.
- **📦 Portable Configuration Profiles** — Export your setup and share it with friends, or maintain separate profiles for different worlds.
- **♻️ Self-Healing Watchdog** — Background process that detects stalled modules and restarts them without manual intervention.
- **🎨 Themeable Control Panel** — Light, dark, and high-contrast themes, plus support for custom palettes.
- **📚 Extensive Documentation** — Every module ships with examples, edge-case notes, and troubleshooting guidance.
- **🤝 Community Module Registry** — Discover and integrate third-party modules built by other players.

## 🧩 Module Breakdown

### 1. ResourceHarvester
Monitors your base's stockpiles and dispatches gathering routines when thresholds dip below configurable levels. Supports per-resource rules, priority tiers, and quiet hours.

### 2. PalFeeder
Ensures every Pal receives nutrition on schedule. Handles dietary preferences, illness states, and seasonal food availability.

### 3. BreedingConcierge
Automates the tedious parts of breeding: egg incubation timing, trait inheritance tracking, and multi-generation lineage logs.

### 4. RaidSentinel
Watches for incoming threats and triggers defensive routines. Optionally alerts you in real time so you can join the fray personally.

### 5. CraftingConductor
Queues crafting jobs based on demand forecasts. Prevents overproduction and understocking with a rolling analysis window.

### 6. ExpeditionPlanner
Suggests optimal routes for resource-gathering expeditions, factoring in Pal stamina, terrain, and your current inventory.

### 7. ReportForge
Generates daily, weekly, or custom-range reports in human-readable and machine-parseable formats.

### 8. ProfileManager
Handles configuration profiles, backup rotation, and cross-device synchronization via local file exchange.

## 🚀 Getting Started

1. **Review the documentation** in the `docs/` directory to understand module capabilities and configuration options.
2. **Select a profile** that matches your play style — Conservative, Balanced, or Thorough.
3. **Configure modules** through the control panel or by editing the plain-text configuration files.
4. **Launch the suite** and observe the initial health report.
5. **Adjust as needed** — the suite is designed to be tuned gradually rather than all at once.

Detailed walkthroughs for each module live in the `docs/modules/` folder.
